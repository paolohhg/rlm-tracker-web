# The Heard Betting Method
## A Market-Behavior Betting System

**Core Philosophy:** Sportsbooks are not predicting winners — they are managing risk against professional bettors. When sportsbooks move lines against public betting pressure, it often indicates sharp money forcing the move.

**Key Principle:** Follow the money, not the narrative.

---

## The Signal Ladder

| Tier | Signal | Conditions | Confidence |
|------|--------|------------|------------|
| 1 | Double No-Narrative RLM | RLM + no media narrative + confirmed across multiple books | Highest |
| 2 | No-Narrative RLM | RLM + no obvious injury/news narrative | High |
| 3 | Steam Move | Sudden synchronized line move across multiple books | High |
| 4 | Frozen Line | Heavy public action on one side + line refuses to move | Medium |
| 5 | Watch | Early movement, incomplete confirmation | Low |

---

## Narrative Filter

If a line move has a clear narrative explanation (injury, lineup change, suspension, weather), the signal is **downgraded**. This prevents betting fake sharp signals.

---

## Timing Rules

- **Early moves** → often sharp
- **Late moves** → often public volume
- System monitors: opening line, current line, velocity of movement

---

## Market Behavior Signals Tracked

- **Line Movement** — difference between opening and current spread
- **Velocity** — speed of movement
- **Book Agreement** — how many books move together
- **Resistance** — when books refuse to move despite public action

---

## The Heard Betting Workflow

1. Collect opening lines
2. Monitor live line movement
3. Track public betting percentages
4. Detect RLM patterns
5. Filter out narrative explanations
6. Rank signal strength
7. Select bets based on signal tier

---

## What the System Deliberately Ignores

- Team trends
- Analyst picks
- Social media narratives
- Gut feeling bets
- Historical stats without market confirmation

---

## Objective

Not to predict games.

To identify when professional bettors are influencing sportsbooks — then bet with that movement.

---

## The Betting Cycle (Martingale Recovery)

The Heard Method bets the sharp side for the **1st half**.

| 1H Result | 2H Action | Cycle Record |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| Win | None needed | ✅ 1-0 |
| Loss | Double bet, same sharp side, 2H | — |
| Loss → 2H Win | Recovery complete | ✅ 1-0 |
| Loss → 2H Loss | Full cycle loss | ❌ 0-1 |

**Key rules:**
- Record is tracked at the **cycle** level, not the individual half
- A 1H loss recovered by a 2H win counts as **1-0** (not 1-1)
- Only a full 2-half sweep counts as **0-1**
- The 2H bet is always the **same sharp side** — never flip
- 2H bet size = 2x the 1H bet (martingale — covers the loss + profit)
